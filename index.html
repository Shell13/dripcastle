Privacy Policy for Drip Castle

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Drip Castle (package com.tidepoollab.dripcastle), published by Tide Pool Lab, handles your information.

Short version

Drip Castle has no user accounts and no analytics, and we receive no personal data from it. The free version shows an occasional full-screen ad from Google AdMob. Because the game is made for children, every ad request is marked as directed to children: the ads are not personalised, there is no remarketing, and the game does not use the Android advertising identifier. Buying the full version removes the ads. Your castles never leave your device.

Data we collect

None. Nothing the game does is sent to us, and we have no server that could receive it. The game cannot read your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files, and it does not hold the permission Android requires to read the advertising identifier (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID).

The permissions the game holds are: INTERNET, which the ads need; ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, which reports whether the device is online; com.android.vending.BILLING, which lets the Google Play Store app offer the purchase described below; and WAKE_LOCK and FOREGROUND_SERVICE, used by a standard Android component that Google's ad library relies on to schedule its network requests.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your castles and your settings to its own private storage on your device so you can return to them later. Next to them it keeps a few counters — how many times the game has been started, how many ads have been shown and when the last one was — so that ads stay rare. None of this leaves the device, it is not readable by other apps, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

Who serves the ads. Google AdMob, an ad network that takes part in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program.

When. Only at natural breaks — after you clear the beach, after you save a new castle for the first time, or when you switch to another castle. Never while you are building, never in the first session, and never more often than once every few minutes.

How the ads are restricted. Every ad request is marked as directed to children and limited to content rated suitable for general audiences. Under that setting Google shows only non-personalised ads, uses no remarketing and does not send the advertising identifier.

What Google's ad library collects. To deliver an ad, count it and prevent fraud, Google's ad library collects your device's IP address (which can show a general location), your interactions with the ads (for example that an ad was shown or tapped), diagnostic information about the ad library's performance, and the app set ID — an identifier Android gives to apps from one publisher on one device, which is not the advertising identifier. This data is encrypted in transit and goes to Google, not to us. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. The game uses Google's consent tool. Because every player is treated as a child, it does not ask for consent to personalised ads — there are none. If Google ever does show you its consent message, you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings → Privacy.

Removing the ads. The full version, bought once through Google Play, removes all ads.

In-app purchases

Drip Castle offers one optional purchase that unlocks the full version of the game. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The game never sees your card number, billing address or any other payment detail, and no payment screen belongs to us.

To unlock what you bought, the game receives from Google Play only the status of the purchase — which product was bought and a token that identifies the order. It keeps that on your device, in the same private storage as your castles, and asks Google Play for it again when you reinstall the game so that your purchase is restored. This information is not sent to us.

Your purchase history is held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Google Play.

Third parties

The only third party that receives data from the game is Google — through AdMob as described above, and through Google Play for the purchase. The game contains no analytics SDKs and no social media integrations. Unity, the engine the game is built with, is configured not to send usage statistics.

Children

Drip Castle is made for children as well as adults. It is rated for all ages, its declared audience on Google Play includes children under 13, and it follows Google Play's Families policy.

We collect no personal information from children, because we collect none from anyone. There are no accounts to create and no name or email to enter. The game treats every player as a child: its ads are child-directed and non-personalised, and it does not use the advertising identifier. The data Google's ad library collects is listed under Advertising above; it goes to Google, not to us.

Apart from the ads, the game has no links out to other apps or websites, and no social features or chat. A parent can remove the ads by buying the full version, and can require approval for every purchase using Google Family Link; the game shows the purchase as awaiting approval until the parent decides.

If you are a parent and have a question about this game, write to us at the address below.

Data collected by Google Play

Google Play collects certain information when you download or run an app — for example install statistics and crash reports. That collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how it handles data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be reflected in the app's Data Safety information on Google Play.
