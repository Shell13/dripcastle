Privacy Policy for Drip Castle

Last updated: 5 September 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Drip Castle (package com.tidepoollab.dripcastle), published by Tide Pool Lab, handles your information.

Short version

Drip Castle does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access at all.

Data we collect

None. The app requests no permissions that would allow it to read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files or device identifiers. The application does not hold the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send information anywhere even in principle.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your castles and your settings to its own private storage on your device so you can return to them later. This data never leaves the device, is not readable by other apps, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Third parties

The game contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs and no social media integrations. No data is shared with anyone.

Children

Drip Castle is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Data collected by Google Play

Google Play collects certain information when you download or run an app — for example install statistics and crash reports. That collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that handle data — for example in-app purchases or advertising — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be reflected in the app's Data Safety information on Google Play.
