Privacy Policy for Drip Castle

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This policy explains how the Android game Drip Castle (package com.tidepoollab.dripcastle), published by Tide Pool Lab, handles your information.

Short version

Drip Castle does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The game itself does not connect to the internet; the optional purchase described below is handled by the Google Play Store app, not by the game.

Data we collect

None. The app requests no permissions that would allow it to read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files or device identifiers. The application does not hold the Android INTERNET permission, so it cannot send information anywhere even in principle. The only permissions it holds are com.android.vending.BILLING, which lets the Google Play Store app offer you the purchase described below, and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, which reports whether the device is online.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your castles and your settings to its own private storage on your device so you can return to them later. This data never leaves the device, is not readable by other apps, and is deleted when you uninstall the game.

In-app purchases

Drip Castle offers one optional purchase that unlocks the full version of the game. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The game never sees your card number, billing address or any other payment detail, and no payment screen belongs to us.

To unlock what you bought, the game receives from Google Play only the status of the purchase — which product was bought and a token that identifies the order. It keeps that on your device, in the same private storage as your castles, and asks Google Play for it again when you reinstall the game so that your purchase is restored. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Your purchase history is held by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. Refunds and payment questions are handled by Google Play.

Third parties

The game contains no advertising networks, no analytics SDKs and no social media integrations. No data is shared with anyone.

Children

Drip Castle is made for children as well as adults. It is rated for all ages, its declared audience on Google Play includes children under 13, and it follows Google Play's Families policy.

We collect no personal information from children, because we collect none from anyone. There are no accounts to create, no name or email to enter, no analytics, and no advertising identifier is read or used. Nothing a child does in the game is transmitted anywhere, so there is nothing for us to store, share or sell, and no data for a parent to request or have deleted — we hold none.

The game contains no advertising, no links out to other apps or websites, and no social features or chat. The one purchase it offers is made through Google Play, and a parent can require approval for every purchase using Google Family Link; the game shows the purchase as awaiting approval until the parent decides.

If you are a parent and have a question about this game, write to us at the address below.

Data collected by Google Play

Google Play collects certain information when you download or run an app — for example install statistics and crash reports. That collection is performed by Google, not by us, and is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that handle data — for example advertising — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be reflected in the app's Data Safety information on Google Play. Because this is a game for children, any advertising it ever carries will come from an ad network certified for families, will not be personalised, and will not use an advertising identifier.

The in-app purchase described above arrived in version 0.8.2. This policy was updated for it, and for the children's audience, on 24 September 2026.
